## 1 · My error

`29` marked Ferronato as "the only case carrying no measurable result" and
suggested demoting it from first position.

**Wrong, and wrong in a specific way.** I applied a *performance marketing*
yardstick — ranking keywords, organic sessions — to a *brand launch*. Search
visibility is the correct measure for Forrey & Galland and KGS. It is the
wrong measure for a category-first luxury launch, where the outcomes are
coverage, distribution and awareness.

This is the same category error as the Diwali framing in `16`: letting a
search figure stand in for work that search was never measuring. Twice now,
which suggests a standing rule rather than a one-off:

> **Match the yardstick to the brief.** A launch is measured in coverage and
> doors. A demand programme is measured in sessions and conversions. Reporting
> one against the other's scale understates the work.
