## 2. Forrey & Galland — the detail

**Keyword portfolio** (UAE): 75 (Jan 24) → 90 (Dec 24) → 95 (Aug 25) → 97 (Feb 26) →
**254 (Aug 26)**. Steepest climb is most recent: +162% in six months from Feb 2026.

**Estimated organic traffic** (UAE): 842 (Jan 24) → peak **1,510 (Aug 25)** → 295 (Aug 26).

**The divergence from Feb 2026 matters.** Keywords rise steeply while traffic falls.
Three plausible explanations, not separable from this data: Semrush's recent months are
routinely incomplete and revise upward; long-tail expansion adding many low-position
keywords; or AI Overviews absorbing clicks, a pattern visible across content-led sites
through 2025–26.

> **Use the keyword figure, date it, and do not quote the Aug 2026 traffic number.**
> The keyword series is robust. The traffic series needs client analytics to read
> honestly — worth asking them for.

### Organic traffic by market, Aug 2026

| Market | Visits/mo | Keywords |
|---|---:|---:|
| **Saudi Arabia** | **511** | 14 |
| United Arab Emirates | 295 | 254 |
| Germany | 117 | 2 |
| Germany (mobile) | 90 | 1 |
| Kuwait | 8 | 4 |
| United States | 3 | 25 |

**The strategic finding: the largest organic audience is not in the UAE.** A Dubai
maison whose biggest search audience sits in **Saudi Arabia** — 511 vs 295 — from just
14 keywords (36 visits per keyword, against roughly 1 in the UAE). High-volume head
terms, not long-tail. Germany third at 117 is unexplained and worth investigating.

This is the most commercially valuable item in the pull, and it is **an insight the
client would pay for** rather than a credentials point. Presenting it back demonstrates
capability better than any traffic figure.

## 3. Galland'or — do not claim

`gallandor.com` has **zero estimated organic traffic in every month across 2024–2026**,
with 0–7 ranking keywords (best month: 7, Nov 2024; currently 0).

**Must not appear as an ecommerce or performance proof point.** Naming "two
direct-to-consumer webshops" implies two trading storefronts; a prospect who checks
finds one performing site and one search engines barely see. Cheap, avoidable
credibility loss on a slide whose purpose is verifiability.

**What it legitimately is:** a product-brand site for Galland'or (non-alcoholic sparkling
apple elixir with 24-carat gold leaf). Describe as brand architecture — true and
defensible — not as commerce.

## 4. KGS and Ferronato

### India, 2024–2026
17 monthly visits (Jan 24) → peak **213 (Jul 25)** → ~96 (Aug 26). **5.6× over the
window.** Ranking keywords fell 45 → 5 across the same period: fewer terms carrying far
more traffic each — consolidation onto high-intent head terms, not decline.

### Global footprint through search
Organic presence in **23 regional databases**. Netherlands leads at 657 visits/mo, then
Portugal 168, Australia 158, **India 96**, Brazil 63, US 56.

Corroborates the public physical footprint: 20+ sales and distribution offices, six
manufacturing sites in Switzerland, Netherlands, Hungary, Portugal, **the UAE and
India**. Bangalore branch opened 2017.

**Connective fact worth using:** KGS manufactures in both the UAE and India. An agency
running marketing for the Middle East and India is covering **the group's two newest
manufacturing bases** — a more interesting frame than either market alone.

### The Ferronato consumer brand has no search footprint
`ferronato.com`: **zero organic traffic in every market.** A handful of keywords in
Argentina, Italy, Switzerland, Brazil, Colombia, Mexico — none driving visits. The
Italian/LatAm skew suggests this may not be the KGS smart-accessories brand at all;
the brand's own domain could not be confirmed.

**Consequence: Ferronato is a launch and brand-system case, not an ecommerce case.**
Present what is externally documented — the Swiss Pavilion debut at Expo 2020 Dubai,
January 2022 (Khaleej Times, BusinessWire, Zawya, Textile World); the first retail move
in a 116-year history; the brand system across product, film and boutique. Strong story.
A commerce claim attached to it is not supportable.

## 5. Limits

- **Semrush estimates of organic visibility, not measured sessions.** Right basis for a
  public claim precisely because reproducible.
- **Traffic Analytics unavailable** — the Semrush plan on this account does not include
  it via MCP, so total visits, channel mix and engagement could not be pulled. Plans:
  https://www.semrush.com/analytics/traffic/trends-api
- **Correlation, not attribution.** Phrase as what happened during the engagement, not
  what EOV caused. The honest form is the more credible one.
- **Client sites not inspectable** — forreyandgalland.com, gallandor.com and eov.ae are
  all blocked by this environment's network policy.
- **Recent months revise.** Date every figure; prefer keywords over traffic for print.
