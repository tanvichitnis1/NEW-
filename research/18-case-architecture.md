## 2. The finding — KGS and Ferronato are one relationship spanning B2B and B2C

**KGS MetaFab®** — metallised fabric of high-tensile polyester, aramid, copper mesh and
non-woven substrates coated with nickel or copper. **Shielding 40–90 dB**, attenuating
EMF/EMI/RF at material level, no power or enclosure required.

**Industrial applications:** naval vessels · aircraft wiring systems · secure courtrooms
· industrial installations. Creates floating Faraday cages, protects communication and
navigation systems, supports secure architecture.

**The same fabric** lines a Ferronato clutch, blocking WiFi, Bluetooth, GPS, GSM and
RFID — including contactless scanning of cards and passports.

> **A 116-year-old industrial manufacturer took a fabric that shields naval vessels and
> made it the lining of a clutch.** That is the case study, and it proves B2B and B2C
> from a single relationship.

The proposition is **physical**, which is what made it provable — privacy demonstrated by
putting a phone in the bag and watching it leave the network.

## 3. The whitespace was positioning, not product

| | |
|---|---|
| Faraday bag market, 2025 | **USD 136.26m** → 235.65m by 2032, CAGR 8.14% |
| Shielding | 40–90 dB at material level |
| Years before KGS entered retail | **116** |
| Riviera Backpack | **$2,100** |

Incumbents — **SLNT / Silent Pocket, GoDark, The Faraday Bag, Batten** — are tactical and
utility: weatherproof nylon, survivalist and security framing, priced accordingly.

Ferronato entered as **"the world's first luxury accessories range with inbuilt privacy
technology"** — Italian leather, Swiss engineering, a Business line framed as *privacy
for professionals*, brand line *crafting privacy*.

**The category existed; the whitespace was positioning.** Every incumbent sold protection
to people who felt threatened. Ferronato sold discretion to people who felt watched. Same
technology, entirely different buyer. **That is a market-entry decision made before
spend — the case demonstrates the method rather than illustrating it.**

**Do not quote market size loosely.** One source: global Faraday bag market USD 136m
(2025). Another: North America alone USD 1.2bn (2024). Different definitions — almost
certainly RFID-blocking accessories broadly vs Faraday bags specifically. Name the source
or use neither.

## 5. Galland'or is brand architecture, not a second storefront

75cl sparkling apple elixir with **23-carat edible gold flakes**; sugar-free,
alcohol-free, no preservatives. Rose and Apple & Raspberry variants. Positioned for
weddings, birthdays, baby showers and corporate events. Sold through both
`forreyandgalland.com` and its own `gallandor.com`.

**The structure is deliberate: Forrey & Galland is the occasion gift, Galland'or is the
occasion toast.** Both calendar-driven, both gifting-led, serving the same moments — one
exists because the other opened the door to them.

**The search finding stands:** `gallandor.com` records zero organic traffic in every
month of 2024–2026. **Present as brand extension, never as a performing storefront.** The
first is true and provable; the second collapses on one check.
