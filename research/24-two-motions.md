---

## 1 · The split

One deck was being designed to serve both. It cannot — and in one of them the
deck barely matters.

| | **Motion A · Tendering** | **Motion B · Cold outbound** |
|---|---|---|
| **Reader** | An evaluator marking your submission against a published rubric alongside six others. Not persuaded — scoring. | A marketing director or owner who did not ask to hear from you. Under a minute of attention. |
| **Targets** | Government, semi-government, large institutions | Private B2B, B2C, D2C |
| **What wins** | Completion certificates, ICV score, rubric-mirrored headings, named team CVs, risk and resourcing | One specific true observation about *their* marketing, plus one case from *their* sector |
| **Role of the deck** | **An annex.** Almost no scoring weight. | **A leave-behind after they reply.** Never the opener. |

**The only genuinely shared asset is the case library.** Everything else is
built separately.

---

## 2 · Motion A — what actually scores

UAE government tenders score sealed envelopes separately (technical,
financial, ICV); **failing any one eliminates the bid before commercial review.**
Technical criteria recur as: approach and methodology, number and value of
similar completed projects **with client completion certification as proof**,
timeline, resource deployment, risk mitigation.

### 2.1 The reference library outranks the deck
"Similar completed projects, evidenced by completion certificates" is a scored
line item. EOV claims 32+ government projects. **If certificates cannot be
produced, that criterion scores near zero however good the work was.**

Administrative task with direct point value: per past project — client name,
scope, value, dates, signed completion certificate or reference letter.
Unglamorous, and worth more tender points than every slide in this programme.

### 2.2 ICV is 20–40% of the total score
Most UAE government tenders since the 2018 programme weight In-Country Value
at a fifth to two-fifths of the whole evaluation. An uncertified bidder
competes for the remainder against certified bidders. **EOV's ICV status
remains unconfirmed** and is the single largest tender lever available — a
finance and procurement task, not a creative one.

### 2.3 The one surviving finding from `20`–`22`
Where a rubric is published, **method statement headings must mirror it
exactly.** Proprietary or generic headings force evaluators to map content to
criteria themselves and consistently lose marks. A tender submission is
restructured to the buyer's words every time — never the credentials deck with
a new cover.

## 3 · Motion B — outbound, and the asset EOV already has

A fifteen-slide deck attached to a cold email does not get opened. Generic
credentials do not earn a reply — every agency emailing that director this
week is also integrated, experienced and results-driven.

**What earns a reply is knowing something specific and true about their
business before writing.** EOV has already demonstrated it can produce this.

### The audit is the opener
The Forrey & Galland work in `15` found, from a search pull alone:
- one product page carrying **59% of all site traffic**
- the brand name cannibalised across **nine competing URLs**
- an unredirected `http://www` duplicate absorbing roughly **11% of visits**

Each is specific, client-verifiable in minutes, and immediately worth money to
fix. That is a repeatable engine: ~20 minutes per prospect produces one real
finding.

**Sequence:** run the pull → open with the single most valuable finding → one
line on who EOV is → one sector-matched case → ask for twenty minutes, not a
capabilities meeting. Deck goes out only after they reply.

**To build:** three sector one-pagers, each carrying one case — luxury and
retail · food and consumer · industrial B2B. Not the deck cut down; a single
page built to be read cold.

## 4 · Case library — finished and sector-tagged

Motion B leads with the *matched* case, not the best one.

### Ferronato — `Luxury` `D2C` `Export` `Market entry`
A 116-year-old industrial manufacturer took a fabric that shields naval
vessels and made it the lining of a $2,100 clutch. Every incumbent sold
protection to people who felt threatened; Ferronato sold discretion to people
who felt watched. Same technology, different buyer.
**Why it works:** the proposition is physical, so provable rather than
asserted. The positioning decision preceded spend — demonstrates the method
rather than illustrating it.
**Leads for:** luxury goods, fashion and accessories, premium D2C, manufacturers
going direct to consumers.

### KGS · MetaFab® — `Industrial B2B` `Technical` `Export` `Long cycle`
Metallised fabric shielding 40–90 dB at material level, no power or enclosure
required. Naval vessels, aircraft wiring, secure courtrooms. Marketed across
India and the Middle East; organic visibility 17 → 96 ranking keywords.
**Why it works:** answers the objection every technical B2B prospect raises —
*our product is too complex for a marketing agency to understand.*
**Leads for:** manufacturing, industrial products, engineering,
defence-adjacent, technical exporters, specification-led sales cycles.

### Forrey & Galland — `Retail` `Food` `Ecommerce` `Seasonal`
Organic visibility 75 → 254 ranking keywords (3.4×) with **zero paid search**,
peaking at 1,510 monthly visits. Occasion-led commerce across Ramadan, Eid
al-Fitr, Eid al-Adha and Diwali, each supported by production, events and
campaign work search never sees (`16`).
**Why it works:** growth without media spend is the most persuasive number in
the library — competitors selling media cannot claim it.
**Leads for:** retail, F&B, hospitality, UAE consumer brands, occasion-driven
calendars.

### The EOV platform — `Conditional`
**Hold until eov.ae is fixed.** The only case a reader can audit while
reading — powerful, and a liability while the site says "digital media and
technology agency" against a deck saying integrated marketing agency (`17`).

### The gap — no nameable government case
Four of five segments have a real case. Government does not, and it is the one
segment whose buying route is Motion A, which scores past projects explicitly.
Until a government engagement can be named or a certificate produced, **the
deck should say "government entities" plainly and stop implying a roster it
cannot show.** An implied claim that cannot be evidenced is worse than a
modest one that can.

## 5 · Core narrative — rewritten (replaces `10` §2)

> Most companies in the UAE buy marketing in pieces. A brand agency here, a
> media buyer there, a web developer, a PR firm, a production house. Each does
> its part competently. Nobody is answerable for whether the whole thing worked.
>
> EOV has worked the other way since 2006. We are an Emirati-founded integrated
> marketing agency in Abu Dhabi and Dubai, with strategy, brand, digital
> platforms, communications, media and production under one roof and one
> accountable team.
>
> That matters most where someone has to justify the spend — an owner spending
> their own money, a finance director asking what a campaign returned, a board,
> a government entity answerable publicly. So we agree what success looks like
> before anything is bought, set the measurement up before anything launches,
> and build every platform and account in the client's name.
>
> Twenty years of it, across luxury goods, retail and food, industrial
> manufacturing, and government.

**What changed.** No government lead. No model name. The tracking discipline
survives as one clause in paragraph three — stated as how EOV works, carrying
no proof burden. Nobody asks for records to substantiate a sentence phrased
that way.

**Check needed:** the disciplines in paragraph two must match what is genuinely
in-house. If production or media is partnered, say so — it costs nothing, and
a buyer who finds out later holds it against you.
