Extracted 1 September 2026. Source: live site, www.eov.ae. Footer reads "all rights reserved @ eov 2022"; sitemap last-modified date is 25 September 2024.

## 1. The headline finding

**The website cannot close the visual assets gap.** There are no case studies on the site, no project write-ups, no client stories, and no video of any kind on any page. What exists is 76 images, most of them stock photography and interface decoration, plus one unlabelled gallery on the About page and one unlabelled gallery on the Digital Presence page.

The images that do look like real EOV work are in those two galleries, roughly 25 to 30 files, with no captions, no client attribution and no dates. They were uploaded at whatever size the Wix editor was given, so some will be usable on a slide and some will not.

So item 1 on your list stays blocking. It is a file transfer from EOV, not a scrape.

Two better sources than the public site, both faster:

1. **The Wix Media Manager on EOV's own account.** It holds the original uploads at full resolution, with upload dates, and usually with more files than were ever placed on a page. One login, and it is the authoritative source. Ask Mohamed or whoever holds the Wix account.
2. **The delivery archive.** Event photography, campaign shots and platform screens almost never live on a marketing site. They sit in whoever's drive ran the projects.

Worth one direct question to Mohamed: which of these were contracted to EOV directly, which were subcontracted, and which are on that wall because someone liked the logo.

## 4. Claims and numbers on the site

Three, all from the About page. All are on-site claims with no source attached, so each needs the same treatment as the award.

- **32+** "Accomplishing over 32 Key Government and Private sector project in innovation technology and Digital Identity Transformation"
- **400+** "With our expanding teams and partnerships, we put at your disposal a Large number of expertise and talents to fulfill your project needs"
- **Best Marketing & Promotion Business Award**, shown next to a trophy graphic, with no issuer and no year

Two notes.

The 400+ figure is the one to watch. It reads as headcount at a glance and the copy quietly qualifies it as "teams and partnerships", which is not the same thing. That is exactly the question buyers ask in the first meeting, and gap 3 on your list. If the real answer is a small core team plus a partner bench, the deck is better off saying that plainly than inheriting a number that will not survive a follow-up question.

The award appears on the site with no more documentation than it has in the deck. So the website does not resolve gap 4 either. Either EOV produces the certificate, or it comes out.

### Innovation Consultation

Deliverables listed: conceptualisation, constructing idea, idea presentation, idea analysis report, alignment with strategic goals, project brief, system design, business requirement documents, requirement RFPs, technical project management, periodic progress reports, testing and evaluation reports.

### Digital Solutions

Deliverables listed: customer journey design, UX design, prototyping, data modelling, information architecture, solution development, system integrations, solution monitoring, solution consultation, development plans, tech product management, technical support management, advisory service.

### Digital Presence
Four elements: Digital Identity, Digital Marketing, Content Creation, Content Management.

**Note for the deck.** "Videography / Animations" and "Virtual Reality" are advertised as services. If EOV sells video, the absence of a single video asset on its own site is the first thing a sharp buyer notices.

## 7. Live liabilities on the site

These are on the public site today, findable by any prospect who Googles EOV before a meeting.

1. **The Careers page is an unedited Wix template.** It reads "We're based in San Francisco's innovation hub" and "Changing the future of transportation means thinking differently." Four job posts sit under it, all located San Francisco, CA: Electrical Engineer, Data Scientist, Artificial Intelligence Researcher, Deep Learning Engineer. Open any of them and the body copy is raw Wix placeholder text, "I'm a paragraph. Click here to add your own text and edit me", under headings reading "What You'll Do" and "Who You Are". None of this is EOV. It is stock template copy that was never replaced, and it has been live for at least two years.
2. **Typos in headline copy.** "ONE HAND DOSE NOT CLAP" as a section header. "YORU DIGITAL PLANS" on Digital Presence. "TECHNOLOHGY" on Digital Solutions. "an eye of an Expect" for expert. "Business Inquires" on the contact form. "your product and servicer". "hit changing to Identify its technical format".
3. **Copyright reads 2022.** Four years stale on a page that claims to help clients thrive in the digital world.

My view: this is a bigger commercial risk than anything on your gap list, because it costs nothing to check and it undercuts the deck before the deck is opened. The Careers page and the four template job posts should be unpublished this week, whatever happens to the rest of the site. That is a five minute job in the Wix editor.

Files supplied alongside this document:

- `eov_image_urls.txt` — the same URLs as a plain list
- `download_eov_assets.sh` — free script that downloads all 76 at original resolution

Every URL in the manifest has the Wix resizing parameters stripped, so what downloads is the original upload rather than the shrunk version the page displays. Expect a wide spread of quality. Wix does not upscale, so anything uploaded small stays small.

**Videos: none.** No Wix-hosted video, no YouTube embed, no Vimeo embed, on any of the 12 pages.

One caveat on completeness. Wix loads galleries lazily, so a handful of images sitting deep in a slider may not have appeared in the page source at fetch time. The Media Manager route in section 1 is the only way to be certain nothing was missed.

- **4. The award.** Not answered. The site shows it with no issuer and no year, exactly as the deck does. Still five minutes, and still binary.
