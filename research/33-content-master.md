## 5 · Case 2 — KGS and Ferronato, one group

**Client-confirmed: two brands, same group.** The only case that
*demonstrates* range rather than claiming it.

### KGS · MetaFab® — External
Metallised fabric (high-tensile polyester, aramid, copper mesh, non-woven
substrates; nickel or copper coated). Attenuates EMF/EMI/RF **at material
level: 40–90 dB**, no power, no enclosure. Naval vessels · aircraft wiring ·
secure courtrooms · industrial installations. **17 → 96 ranking keywords
(5.6×)** across India and the Middle East.

### Ferronato · the launch — External
*"The world's first luxury accessories range with inbuilt privacy technology."*
Swiss family, **fourth generation**, **30+ years supplying privacy technology
to defence, aeronautics and aerospace**. Sandro and Alessia Ferronato. Italian
leather, made in Italy. Blocks WiFi, Bluetooth, GPS, GSM, RFID.

- **300,000+** visitors at the Expo 2020 Dubai debut
- **May 2023** first Swiss boutique
- **US + EU** distribution expanding
- AW2025 trading · Riviera Backpack **$2,100** · Business and Women's lines
- Swiss Luxury Magazine editorial
- ferronato-switzerland.com · ferronato.swiss

### The positioning decision — the actual story
Every incumbent (SLNT/Silent Pocket, GoDark, The Faraday Bag, Batten) sold
**protection to people who felt threatened**. Ferronato sold **discretion to
people who felt watched**. Same technology, different buyer. **The category
existed; the whitespace was positioning** — decided before spend.

### Measure it correctly
Ferronato shows ~83 organic visits/month across 10 markets. **Category norm for
luxury DTC, not a failure** — search was never the channel. **Do not quote
market size loosely:** one source says global Faraday bag market USD 136m
(2025), another says North America alone USD 1.2bn (2024). Different
definitions.

**Still needed:** publication list with dates and tier · retail doors ·
awareness study · influencer reach · which markets US/EU covers.

## 11 · Market context — informs, does not appear

- **Breadth is a commodity claim.** ~1,600 UAE agencies; Boopin states the
  one-accountable-relationship claim verbatim at larger scale.
- **Credentials buyers ask three things:** can you do this, have you done it,
  will you be straightforward. None asks for a methodology — hence no branded model.
- **Agencies keep credentials generic** because confidentiality gives no choice.
- **Tenders are a separate motion.** Headings mirror the buyer's rubric; ICV is
  20–40% of total; completion certificates are a scored line item. The deck is
  an annex there.
- **Arabic gap:** ~63% prefer Arabic content, under 20% of sites optimised.
- **Ad-account ownership is a live grievance** — Meta pushes ownership to
  whoever pays the invoice, usually the agency.
