## 1. The correction

`15` reported **"Diwali: one landing page, one keyword, one visit a month."** As a
measure of the *work*, that is wrong, and it should have been caveated rather than left
to stand as if it described the engagement.

**Search data measures discoverability, not delivery.** It cannot see a shoot day, a set
build, a window install, a media dinner, a corporate preview, a packaging structure or a
boutique dressed for a season. A campaign can be the largest production of the year and
register as a single URL.

Nine workstreams feed one occasion. Search sees one of them.

---

## 2. The finding the error uncovered

> **A full production runs for each occasion. A single thin page survives it.
> The investment happens; the compounding asset does not.**

Every season: concept developed, collection designed, packaging engineered, shoot
produced, boutique dressed, event run, creators briefed. The occasion passes and what
remains discoverable is one URL ranking for one term.

**EOV's own deck names this exact failure** under "the problem":
*"Assets that do not connect. Each defensible alone. Incoherent together."* and
*"Spend nobody can trace."* Here is a measured live instance of both, in a client's data,
on work EOV delivered.

**Why this is the strongest version of the case study:** it lets EOV present the work at
full scale *and* name what the industry does wrong with it — including where EOV's own
remit stopped. More credible than a case study claiming everything went perfectly.

It also sets up the sale. The Ramadan corporate gifting page already ranks **#8 for
"ramadan hampers"** and **#3 for "eid gift uae"** — the production quality is evidently
there. What is missing is the layer that turns each season's production into a durable
compounding asset. **That is what Always-On is for, and the argument for a retainer over
a project.**

## 3. The calendar mechanics — why this season is hard

Ramadan, Eid al Fitr and Eid al Adha are the spine of the year and behave unlike the
other occasions.

- **The season moves.** Ramadan began ~11 March 2024, ~1 March 2025, ~18 February 2026 —
  nearly three weeks earlier in two years, drifting ~11 days annually. An agency planning
  to a fixed Gregorian calendar is late before it starts, and packaging and print lead
  times do not move with it. *(Dates approximate; the Islamic calendar is lunar and
  confirmed by moon sighting.)*
- **Two of the three run back to back.** Ramadan is ~30 days and Eid al Fitr lands the
  day it ends. Not two campaigns — one continuous ~6-week run in which the second peak
  must be shot, printed and in-store before the first has finished. Eid al Adha follows
  ~2 months later.
- **The registers differ.** Ramadan is reflective, gifting quiet and familial. Eid is
  celebratory, gifting outward and social. Eid al Adha different again. The same
  collection cannot simply be recoloured across all three.

**This is the detail that proves the work.** Anyone can list occasions. Knowing the
calendar drifts, that the two biggest peaks overlap in production, and that the registers
differ is what an agency that has actually run the season knows.

## 4. The capture grid

EOV to fill from its own files, once per occasion. **Every figure is EOV's own record,
defendable without client permission** — it counts what EOV did, not what the client earned.

| Workstream | What to count |
|---|---|
| Concept & art direction | Themes developed · routes presented (name them — "Majorelle Gardens", "Jardin d'Or") |
| Collection design | SKUs · box formats · flavours |
| Packaging & print | Structures · finishes · corporate variants |
| **Photography** | **Shoot days · set builds · final assets** — the most under-claimed number in most agency decks |
| Film & motion | Films · cuts per platform |
| Retail & environments | Windows · installs · boutiques dressed |
| Events & activations | Events · guests · media dinners · previews |
| Influencer, PR & gifting | Creators · seeding lists · coverage — **only what EOV briefed and ran directly** |
| Digital & commerce | Landing pages · emails · social assets · webshop merchandising |

**Per-occasion tally** — one row per occasion per year:

*Lunar calendar (moves ~11 days earlier annually):* Ramadan · Eid al Fitr · Eid al Adha
*Planned independently:* Diwali · Easter · UAE National Day

Columns: Concept / SKUs / Shoot days / Films / Retail installs / Events / Creators

## 5. What it becomes on the slide

> "Concept, collection, packaging, photography, film, retail, events and commerce —
> six occasions a year, each built once and carried from the kitchen to the shelf to
> the screen."

Plus counted tiles: occasions per year · shoot days · retail installs · events delivered.

**Breadth under discipline, evidenced by count rather than adjective.** No client
permission, no third-party figures, no caveat — and it is the claim the whole deck rests on.

### Two rules while filling it in
1. **Count only what EOV ran.** A partner-produced shoot belongs in the partner's column.
   A shorter honest list beats a longer qualified one.
2. **Every number must survive one follow-up.** "How many shoot days?" invites "which
   ones, and when?" If the files cannot answer, leave it out.
