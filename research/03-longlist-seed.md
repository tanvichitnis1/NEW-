### Structural signals worth carrying forward

- **Integration is the stated battleground, not a niche claim.** WPP publicly reports
  that marketers increasingly want integrated capability over fragmented services, and
  is rebuilding WPP Media to unify media, production, data and commerce. Every major
  holding company is restructuring on the same thesis. *Implication for EOV: "integrated"
  is table stakes language at the top of the market — EOV cannot differentiate on the
  word itself, only on a provable, specific form of it.* (Conf. H)
- **The holding companies are weakened, not dominant.** WPP revenues sliding, Dentsu
  and Omnicom in cost-cutting and restructuring through 2025; Publicis and Havas
  stronger. Consolidation speculation (Publicis/Dentsu). *Implication: incumbent
  disruption creates a genuine opening for stable independents — EOV's ~20-year
  continuity becomes a competitive asset, not just a fact.* (Conf. H)
- **Government diversification agendas are the primary demand driver.** Saudi Vision
  2030 and UAE strategic agendas steering budgets toward tourism, fintech, gaming and
  cultural mega-projects. *Directly favourable to EOV's public-sector base.* (Conf. M)
- **Saudi is rising fast relative to UAE.** Saudi brands and agencies took seven Grand
  Prix at Dubai Lynx 2025; BigTime Creative Shop (Riyadh) placed third for MENA Agency
  of the Year. *Implication: a UAE-only competitive frame is already out of date — the
  GCC scope decision was correct.* (Conf. H)

### Government tender mechanics — highest-value thread

Early findings confirm this workstream's promotion to priority 3:

- UAE government tender documents typically contain **5–15 pages of explicit evaluation
  criteria with published scoring weights** across technical, commercial, ICV, HSE and
  Emiratisation factors. (Conf. M)
- **Technical pre-qualification precedes financial opening** — capability, past
  experience, capacity, certifications and methodology are assessed before price is
  seen. *Implication: the credentials/PQQ document is the gate, and price cannot
  rescue a weak one.* (Conf. M)
- **ICV (In-Country Value) can carry 20–40% of total score** on tenders above AED 5M,
  covering Emirati employment, local sourcing and UAE economic contribution. (Conf. M)
- Live examples of the exact tender type exist (e.g. ADREC RFQ for marketing,
  communication and PR agency services) — real documents to obtain and dissect.

**This is the single most important early finding for the deck.** If ICV, Emiratisation
and local-content scoring carry up to 40% of the evaluation, then EOV's 20-year UAE
presence, local establishment and Emirati employment record are not "about us" content —
they are **scored, weighted line items**. That reframes the entire document. It also
generates an urgent client question: what is EOV's actual ICV certification status?

### Archetype E — Government / public-sector specialists
**Under-mapped and the most important gap.** This is EOV's incumbent home ground and the
archetype where the competitive set is least visible in trade press, because public-sector
work rarely wins creative awards and is often disclosed only through tender records.
Bird and Créo are the two confirmed entries so far. **Priority action: build this
archetype out from government tender award records and supplier registries rather than
from trade media or directories.**
