## 1 · What "sent ahead" changes

Sending the deck ahead of a meeting is common practice and the right instinct.
But it is the **harder** brief, not the easier one.

Most agency credentials decks are built to be **presented** — sparse slides, a
photograph, three words, the presenter carrying the meaning. Sent ahead, that
deck reads as empty. The reader supplies nothing, asks nothing, and stops the
moment it stops making sense.

Five rules follow:

1. **Every headline is a full sentence carrying the point.** "Ferronato" tells
   a solo reader nothing. "A 116-year-old industrial manufacturer wanted to
   sell to consumers for the first time" tells them enough to keep going.
2. **The first three slides do most of the work.** Assume many readers stop
   there. Who EOV is, what the problem is, what EOV does about it — all land
   before slide four.
3. **Skimmable in two minutes, readable in eight.** Headlines alone tell the
   whole story; body copy rewards the slower reader and never carries a point
   the headline omitted.
4. **PDF, never PowerPoint.** Opens on a phone, keeps its fonts, cannot be
   edited or lifted. Under 10MB or it will not arrive. Name it
   `EOV-Credentials-2026.pdf` — the filename sits in the inbox for months.
5. **One deck, two uses.** Built to read alone, it presents fine — you talk
   over slides that already make sense. Do not maintain two versions. For
   genuinely cold approaches use the sector one-pager (`24` §3).

## 3 · Four unverified claims — check before this goes to anyone

Each is written as fact in the draft, each is checkable in minutes, and each
is the kind a prospect can catch.

| Slide | Claim | Question |
|---|---|---|
| 02 | "Offices in Abu Dhabi and Dubai" | Two working offices, or one office and a registered address? |
| 04 | Six disciplines | Which are genuinely in-house? Anything partnered must say so. |
| 05 | "We build in your name" | **Easiest claim here to disprove.** True today, or it comes out. |
| 15 | Paid definition stage | Real practice, or an intention? |

Everything else rests on evidence already in this programme: Ferronato and KGS
product and technical detail from published sources (`18`); Forrey & Galland
and KGS figures from Semrush pulls, September 2026 (`14`, `15`). Attribution
of that growth to EOV rests on the client's own statement.

## 4 · The gap

**Images.** As it stands this is a well-argued document about work nobody can
see. Slides 08 and 10 in particular are written for photography that does not
yet exist in this programme — product, campaign, boutique, atelier, shoot
stills, event coverage.

Search data measures whether people could *find* the work. Photographs are
what show the work itself. A credentials deck without them is a document.
