## 2 · The three craft rules, applied

### 2.1 The claim is made once
Slide 04 states it. It never appears again. Slide 12 echoes it without
restating it, so the reader completes the thought themselves — which is why it
lands. A claim restated is a claim being defended.

### 2.2 Sections end by opening the next
Written transitions, not assumed:

- **03 closes:** *"It works — until something cannot be done twice."* → the turn
- **05 closes:** a logo wall creates one question — *what did they actually do
  for them?* Never summarise this slide; the question is the point
- **07 closes:** *"That is one business. The next is one owner with two."*
- **10 closes:** *"Same owner. Same agency. Two entirely different jobs."* Then
  **no bridge** into 11 — the escalation should be felt, not announced

### 2.3 The last line of the last case is the argument
Not the contact slide. By slide 16 the decision is made; 13–16 are
administration.

## 3 · Six changes from `25`, each with a narrative reason

| Change | Why |
|---|---|
| **Claim slide added at 04** | The document had structure but no hinge. Everything before is setup, everything after is proof |
| **Case order reversed** | Rising stakes, not story quality (`31`). F&G is the familiar shape the reader maps onto their own business; Vienna is the one remembered |
| **KGS + Ferronato adjacent** | Range only demonstrates itself when both halves sit together. Split, it becomes two ordinary cases |
| **Disciplines moved to 13** | A capability list read *before* the proof is a claim; read *after* it, a fact |
| **OPEC from optional to closing** | Now structural — consent is a higher priority than in `27` §6 |
| **Transitions written, not assumed** | Three slides end on a line whose job is to open the next. That join is where a list becomes a document |
