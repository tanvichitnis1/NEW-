**The page carrying 59% of traffic has zero commercial or transactional keywords.**

The site attracts an audience it is not built to convert, and ranks — but does not
surface — for the terms that would convert. **A fixable structural problem, not a
content-volume problem.**

## 4. Emirati Women's Day — the missing occasion

**28 August.** Instituted 2015 by H.H. Sheikha Fatima bint Mubarak; marks the founding of
the General Women's Union in 1975. Described in trade press as one of the most
significant corporate recognition occasions in the UAE calendar.

**No page. No rankings. Absent from the calendar** while Diwali and Easter are present.

**Competitors are active:**
- **Novikov Café** — bespoke boxes of handcrafted chocolates for the occasion
- **BakeMyDay** (Dubai cookie brand) — limited edition with packaging designed by Emirati
  artist **Mariam Al Obeidli**, every tin wrapped in her floral artwork

**The instructive pattern:** commentary is consistent that a generic premium chocolate box
falls flat for this occasion — the gift must carry meaning, not just quality. An artist
collaboration converts confectionery into a cultural object, which is exactly the move a
maison positioned on "edible couture" is built to make.

**Adjacent gaps to check:** Mother's Day (21 March in the Arab world) and the
Christmas/New Year corporate season — neither appears in the ranking set.

## 5. Two technical faults

### Fault one — brand-term cannibalisation
"forrey and galland" (590 vol, the highest-volume term) ranks across **nine URLs**:

| Position | URL |
|---:|---|
| 2 | `/` (homepage) |
| 6 | `/contact/` |
| 14 | `/home/` (duplicate homepage) |
| 25 | `/home/` |
| 27 | `/tag/dubai-mall-chocolates/` |
| 30 | `/orders-and-shipping/` |
| 31 | `/boutique/` |
| 33 | `/praline-chocolate/` |
| 34 | `/the-parisian-macaron-edition/` |

A separate `/home/` page competes with the real homepage. Authority that should
consolidate on one page is spread across nine.

### Fault two — the site ranks twice
`http://www.forreyandgalland.com/` ranks **independently of** the https version and
carries 32 monthly visits — roughly **11% of total organic traffic arriving on a
non-canonical version.** Unresolved redirect/canonical configuration, splitting both
authority and analytics.

Both are diagnosis-grade: specific, verifiable, fixable in days. Neither needs a campaign.

### Arabic
Site ranks **#7 for "جاناش"** (ganache, 390 monthly searches) — the only meaningful
Arabic ranking in the set. Against the market finding that ~63% of UAE internet users
prefer Arabic for local purchasing decisions while under a fifth of Dubai businesses
maintain optimised Arabic content, this is **demand demonstrated and almost entirely
unworked.**

## 6. What this gives the deck

The Forrey & Galland slide can carry **scope, proof and insight** rather than one number:

- **Scope, evidenced:** eight occasion landing pages across six occasions with
  corporate-gifting variants — one calendar through boutique, packaging, campaign and
  owned commerce.
- **Proof, sourced:** keyword coverage up 3.4× on zero paid search; #1 UAE positions on
  the sugar-free cluster; #3 "eid gift uae"; #8 "ramadan hampers".
- **Insight, unprompted:** the missing Emirati Women's Day occasion, the inverted funnel,
  two technical faults — found in the client's own data before anyone asked.

**Anyone can present what they did. Arriving with what the data says to do next is the
difference between a credentials deck and a consultation** — and the practical
demonstration of "decisions before spend."
